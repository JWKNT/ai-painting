The Legend of Zelda: Korok Forest

This is Korok Forest in the Great Hyrule Forest, from The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, a game by Nintendo. The Master Sword stands in a stone platform in front of the Great Deku Tree.

Koroks live in this forest. The white and blue flowers are Silent Princess flowers. The painting has 14,275 brush strokes.
