Tsukihime: The Millennium Castle

This is the Millennium Castle Brunestud, from Tsukihime, a visual novel by Type-Moon. It is the castle of Arcueid, the princess of the True Ancestors. No one built it: she makes it real by her will. In the new version of the story it stands, on one night, in the middle of the city.

The moon is in the name of the story, which means Moon Princess. No one is in the picture. The painting has 8,752 brush strokes.
