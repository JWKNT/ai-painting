Baldr Sky: The Dive

This is the virtual world of Baldr Sky, a visual novel with action by Giga. In it people fight in machines that are called simulacrums. They fight each other, and they fight viruses, which are machines with no one in them.

The white machine is Kagerou, the simulacrum of Kou Kadokura. He is a mercenary, and he looks for the truth about the day that is called Gray Christmas. We see Kagerou from behind as it dives. The painting has 22,895 brush strokes.
