Wonderful Everyday: The Hill of Sunflowers

This is the hill of sunflowers, from Wonderful Everyday (Subarashiki Hibi), a visual novel by KeroQ. Most of the story is dark. One of its ends comes back to this hill in the country, in summer.

Hasaki is on the left, with her rabbit doll. Yuki is on the right. The story speaks of the sky again and again, and of how to live happily. The painting has 33,351 brush strokes.
