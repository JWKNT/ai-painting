Wonderful Everyday: The School Roof

This is the roof of the school in Wonderful Everyday (Subarashiki Hibi), a visual novel by KeroQ. The story comes back to this roof again and again. A prophecy in the story says that the world will end on the twentieth of July, under this sky.

Yuki stands with her back to us. Rain has left water on the floor, and the water holds the sky. The story speaks of the sky again and again, and of how to live happily. The painting has 21,042 brush strokes.
