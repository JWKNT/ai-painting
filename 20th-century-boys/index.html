20th Century Boys: The Resurrection

This is the first day of the Expo of 2015 in 20th Century Boys, a manga by Naoki Urasawa. Friend is the man in the mask who made himself the savior of the world. He is dead, and his coffin stands in a hill of flowers in the stadium.

The Pope has come to pray for him. Then the dead man gets up and stands in front of his coffin. The sign over the flowers and on his mask was the mark of a secret club of small boys. The painting has 31,008 brush strokes.
