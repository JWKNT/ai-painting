Utawarerumono: Above Tuskur

This is the first Utawarerumono, a game by Leaf and Aquaplus. A wounded man with no memory wakes in a village. A white mask is on his face, and it does not come off. Eruruu nurses him, and her grandmother names him Hakuowlo. Later he becomes the emperor of a new country, Tuskur.

He stands above the fortress with Eruruu near sunset. Her sister Aruruu rides ahead on Mukkuru, the white tiger. The painting has 26,193 brush strokes.
