Re:Zero: From Zero

This is the end of episode 18 of Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World, from the novels by Tappei Nagatsuki. Subaru has failed too many times, and he asks Rem to run away with him. She will not. She tells him what she sees in him, and that she loves him.
