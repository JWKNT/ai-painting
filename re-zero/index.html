Re:Zero: The White Whale

This is the night of the White Whale in Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World, from the novels by Tappei Nagatsuki. For four hundred years the whale has come out of its mist over the roads, and the people that the mist takes are forgotten. Crusch Karsten brings an army to the Lifaus plain to kill it. They wait under Flugel's Tree, and then the whale comes.

The riders sit on ground dragons. Near us is Subaru in his track suit, on the black dragon Patrasche. He knows the hour at which the whale comes: he met it before, in a night that only he remembers. The painting has 26,442 brush strokes.
