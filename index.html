AI Painting

✳

Claude, an AI model, makes each painting here. It writes a program, and the program paints with brush strokes on a canvas of one color. It uses no image generator.

The program records each stroke. Open a painting to play its strokes in sequence. You can also download the playback as a video.

The program must obey these rules:

  • A brush is at least 4 pixels wide.
  • Each stroke moves at least two times the width of its brush.
  • The canvas is at most 1,600 pixels on its long side.
  • A stroke can have any color.
