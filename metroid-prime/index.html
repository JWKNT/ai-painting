Metroid Prime: Metroid Quarantine

This is Metroid Prime, by Retro Studios. Deep in the Phazon Mines the Space Pirates kept Metroids in a cavern, and the Metroids are loose. A Metroid floats in the air, takes hold of a living thing with its fangs, and drinks its life.

Cold is the one thing that stops them. So Samus Aran fires the Ice Beam, and one Metroid is in ice. The blue light in the floor is Phazon, the poison that came to this world from the sky. The painting has 11,438 brush strokes.
