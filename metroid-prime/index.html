Metroid Prime: The Phendrana Shorelines

This is Phendrana Drifts, from Metroid Prime, by Retro Studios. Samus Aran comes up out of the caves of fire into the cold. The first thing that she sees is this shore, with the Ice Temple of the Chozo across the water.

The Chozo made the temple long ago and are gone, but their lamps still burn. Samus wears the Varia Suit, and her arm cannon is on her right arm. We see only her back. The painting has 15,723 brush strokes.
