No Game No Life: The Fall into Disboard

This is the start of No Game No Life, the novels by Yuu Kamiya. Sora and Shiro are a brother and a sister who have never lost a game. A god pulls them into his world, and they come out high in its sky and fall.

In this world every quarrel is settled by a game. The pieces at the edge of the world are his, and he sits on the king. To take that piece is to win everything. The painting has 26,606 brush strokes.
