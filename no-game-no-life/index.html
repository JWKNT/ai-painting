No Game No Life: Disboard

This is Disboard, from No Game No Life, the novels by Yuu Kamiya. Sora and Shiro are a brother and a sister who have never lost a game. A god calls them to his world, where every quarrel is settled by a game.

The pieces at the edge of the world are his. The city below is Elkia, the last city of the people who have no magic. We see the two from behind, hand in hand. The painting has 24,766 brush strokes.
