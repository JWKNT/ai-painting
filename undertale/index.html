Undertale: The Last Corridor

This is the last corridor, in Undertale, the game by Toby Fox. A human child has fallen into the land of the monsters, under a mountain. In this game you can end every fight without a blow. You are a small red heart, and you keep it away from what the monsters throw.

At the end waits Sans, a short skeleton who jokes and sleeps at his post. In this hall of gold he judges what you did. If you killed everyone, he fights, with rows of bones and with great skulls that fire light. The painting has 21,046 brush strokes.
