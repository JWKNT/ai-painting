Undertale: The Castle Far Off

This is Waterfall, in Undertale, the game by Toby Fox. A human child has fallen into the land of the monsters, under a mountain. Here it rains from the roof of the cavern. The child has taken an umbrella, and a monster child walks under it too.

Far across the dark, the castle of the king comes into view. The lights overhead are crystals. The monsters cannot see the stars, so they make their wishes on these. The blue flowers are echo flowers, which say again the last thing that they heard. The painting has 11,702 brush strokes.
