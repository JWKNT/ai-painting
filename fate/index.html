Fate/stay night: Unlimited Blade Works

This is Unlimited Blade Works, from Fate/stay night, a visual novel by Type-Moon. It is the world inside the hero Archer, made real: a dry plain, a sky of gears, a hill, and every sword that he has seen.

No one is in it. There are only the swords, and the shadows that they throw toward us. The painting has 14,625 brush strokes.
