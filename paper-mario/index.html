Paper Mario: Boggly Woods

This is Boggly Woods, from Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, by Intelligent Systems. The wood has a gold sky, black trunks and white leaves. The great tree far off is the home of the Punies.

Punio, the small one on the path, came to ask for help, because invaders have taken the tree. Mario goes there by air: in this world people are paper, and he can fold himself into a plane. The painting has 25,932 brush strokes.
