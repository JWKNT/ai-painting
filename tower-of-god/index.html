Tower of God: The First Test

This is the first test of Tower of God, the webtoon by SIU. A boy, Bam, comes into the Tower to look for his friend Rachel. On the first floor he must break a ball. The ball hangs in a space that is full of shinsu, behind a wall of bars, and the White Steel Eel lives there.

He has the Black March, a needle that a princess of the Tower lends him. We see him from far back, small, with his back to us. The painting has 11,533 brush strokes.
