Monster: Ruhenheim

This is the town of Ruhenheim, near the end of Monster, a manga by Naoki Urasawa. It rains, and the street is empty. Johan Liebert stands in the road and points to his forehead.

Doctor Kenzo Tenma aims a pistol at him. Tenma once saved the life of this man. The painting has 7,559 brush strokes.
