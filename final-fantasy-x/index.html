Final Fantasy X: The Ruins of Zanarkand

This is how Final Fantasy X, a game by Square, starts and how it nears its end. The party has made camp above the ruins of Zanarkand, the city that Sin broke a thousand years ago. Tidus leaves the fire, looks at the city, and says: listen to my story.

The lights in the air are pyreflies. The weapons of the party stand together in the rock near us: the sword Brotherhood, Yuna's staff, Kimahri's lance, Auron's blade and jug, Wakka's ball and Lulu's doll. The painting has 16,675 brush strokes.
