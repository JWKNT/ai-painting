Final Fantasy X: The Sending

This is the Sending at Kilika, from Final Fantasy X, a game by Square. Sin has broken the port and killed many of its people. A summoner must send the dead, or they become fiends.

Yuna dances on the water at sunset. The sea lifts her, and the dead leave the water as pyreflies. The people of Kilika watch from the walks, and her guardians are with them on the left. The painting has 8,692 brush strokes.
