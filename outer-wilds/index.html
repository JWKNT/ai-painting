Outer Wilds: The Last Evening

This is a scene from Outer Wilds, a game by Mobius Digital. The sun is a red giant above Timber Hearth. A traveler sits at a campfire on a cliff, next to the ship.

The Hourglass Twins cross the sun. Giant’s Deep, the Attlerock, Brittle Hollow, Dark Bramble and the Interloper are in the sky. The painting has 7,659 brush strokes.
