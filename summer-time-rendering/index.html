Summer Time Rendering: A Shadow on the Beach

This is Summer Time Rendering, the manga by Yasuki Tanaka. On the island of Hitogashima a shadow makes itself a copy of a person and then kills that person. The master of the shadows is Shide, who has four arms and wears black armour.

Ushio is a shadow too. She is the copy of a girl who drowned, and she fights on the side of the living. Her hair is her weapon. She can make it hard, like a blade. The painting has 17,545 brush strokes.
