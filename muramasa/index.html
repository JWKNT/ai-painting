Full Metal Daemon Muramasa: The Two Wheels

This is a duel in the sky from Full Metal Daemon Muramasa, a visual novel by Nitroplus. In it the armour of a warrior is a living thing of steel, a tsurugi, and it flies. Two of them fight as two wheels that touch: each goes round its own circle, and where the circles touch, they strike.

The crimson armour is Muramasa, and Kageaki Minato wears it. The silver light far off is the Silver Star, which he hunts. The painting has 17,552 brush strokes.
