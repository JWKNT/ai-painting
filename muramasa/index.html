Full Metal Daemon Muramasa: The Crimson Spider

This is Full Metal Daemon Muramasa, the visual novel by Nitroplus. A tsurugi is a suit of armour with a mind of its own. When no one wears it, it has the shape of a beast. Muramasa has the shape of a great crimson spider.

The man is Minato Kageaki. When he calls, the spider comes apart and closes on him as his armour. The armour has a law: for each enemy that it kills, it must kill one that he loves. The painting has 14,279 brush strokes.
