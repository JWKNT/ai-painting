Shinsekai Yori: The Impure Cat

This is the impure cat, from Shinsekai Yori (From the New World), the novel by Yusuke Kishi. A thousand years from now, the village of Kamisu 66 is quiet and kind. Every person in it has a power that can kill. So the village watches its children, and a child who may go wrong does not grow up.

The cat is the thing that comes for such a child. The village made it from house cats: it is three meters long, with legs and a neck that are much too long. The children think that it is only a story to make them go home before dark. Here the going-home song plays from the horns at dusk, and one child is late. The painting has 18,155 brush strokes.
