Berserk: The Eclipse

This is the Eclipse, from Berserk, a manga by Kentaro Miura. Griffith led the Band of the Hawk, and he has lost everything. The sun goes black, and he and all his people are in a place that is not in the world.

A great hand lifts him up to the four members of the God Hand, who stand on the ends of its fingers. They wait for the words that give his friends to them. The painting has 29,077 brush strokes.
