Sword Art Online: The End of Aincrad

This is the end of the first story of Sword Art Online, a series of novels by Reki Kawahara. The game is over, and the floating castle Aincrad breaks apart at sunset.

Kirito and Asuna sit on a floor of glass above the clouds and watch the castle fall. The painting has 6,840 brush strokes.
